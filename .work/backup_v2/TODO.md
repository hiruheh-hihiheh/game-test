# Starfall Salvage

## What's in the game

- **60 levels** in 6 sectors, built from fixed seeds so each level always plays the same.
- **9 enemy types** with their own movement: three asteroid sizes (large ones split), fighters, diving raiders, weaving ships, mines that burst, gunships and elites.
- **6 bosses** with three stages and attacks including fans, rings, spirals, bullet curtains, laser strikes and summons.
- **Hangar** with 6 permanent upgrades and a second ship, paid for with salvage.
- **9 module cards** chosen between waves, **Nova Burst** special, combos, stars, and **Endless mode**.
- **Original audio:** 30 sound effects and 6 music tracks, generated by `tools/gen_audio.mjs`.
- **Original visuals:** custom font, 6 sector backdrops, sprites from the Starfall asset pack.
- **Full menus:** title, sector map, hangar, settings, pause, results.
