## What we collect

Starfall Salvage does not collect, store or share any personal information. The game has no accounts of its own, no ads and no analytics.

**[ONLY IF YOU SHIP GOOGLE PLAY GAMES CLOUD SAVE, replace the sentence above with the paragraph below and update the Data Safety form. Until then, keep the sentence above.]**

If you sign in to Google Play Games on Android, the game saves a copy of your progress (levels, currency, ships, gear and similar game data, not your settings) to Google Play Games Saved Games so you can restore it on a new device. Google handles your sign-in and stores that copy under your Google account, under Google's own privacy policy. The game does not receive your name, email address or a sign-in token, and the developer cannot see your saved game. Without Google Play Games, or on a computer, progress stays on your device only.

## What is stored on your device

Your progress and settings are saved on your device only. If you turn on "Save balance log on device" in Settings, the game keeps a small log of level results on your device. It is never sent anywhere.

## Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Deleting your data

Use **Settings > Reset Progress**, or uninstall the game. **[If Google Play Games cloud save ships:]** resetting or uninstalling does not delete the copy stored by Google. It can be deleted from the Google Play Games app (Settings, Delete Play Games account and data) or your Google account's data controls.
